### 2. [AI-Powered Travel Planner](https://github.com/AdityaJadhav17/Travel-Agntcy): distributed multi-agent system
A LangGraph supervisor coordinates agents that plan flights, hotels and activities, behind FastAPI services and a React/TypeScript UI. Containerized with Docker, services talk over NATS, and Grafana/ClickHouse provide observability.

**Impact:** ~40% lower inter-service latency from containerized microservices and NATS messaging. [Demo](https://youtu.be/T0EkJ9J_IQU)

![Stack](https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=python,fastapi,react,ts,docker,grafana&theme=dark)

### 3. [Stockroom](https://github.com/AdityaJadhav17/Stockroom): inventory and purchase approvals
Inventory tracking with role-based purchase approvals and an auditable history of every stock movement, built with C#, ASP.NET Core Razor Pages, EF Core and SQLite. Each stock change and its history record commit in one transaction, and conditional updates stop concurrent requests from double-receiving a delivery or overselling.

**Impact:** 165 integration tests and 11 browser tests, with business rules, authorization, races and rollback all covered.

![Stack](https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=cs,dotnet,sqlite,githubactions&theme=dark)
